## What changed

**Database** (two new migrations, proven with `dev-stack.sh reset`)
- `20261107001000_opening_hours.sql`:
  - **Tables:** `opening_hours` (one row per weekday; closing at least 1 hour after opening and by midnight, stored as 24:00), `closed_dates` (note ≤ 120), and `kitchen_settings` (one row: last orders 0/15/30, closed early today, till number 5–10 digits).
  - **`public.has_permission(...)`:** the single owner check. Every write goes through it, through RLS or the RPCs. 2.3 extends it there.
  - **Open-or-closed decision:** `kitchen_status_at(ts)` / `kitchen_status()` decide it, on Nairobi time. Last orders include their whole minute.
  - **RPCs:** `close_early`, `reopen_today`, `save_opening_hours`, `set_till_number`.
  - **Sold-out regulars:** `dishes.unavailable_on` and `regular_on_sale()`. A regular switched off is off only for that date and comes back on the next day by itself, with no cron or scheduler.
  - **`get_order_status`:** returns `till_number`.
- `20261107001100_place_order_opening_hours.sql`: `place_order` refuses when the kitchen is closed (`kitchen_closed`) and refuses a pickup time outside today's open hours (`pickup_time`). It still reprices from the database.
- **Tests:** `tests/db/opening-hours.test.ts` has 34 tests, one or more per rule (each status reason, midnight, last-orders minute, closed date, closed early and reopen, owner-only writes refused for staff and anon, place_order refusals for every order kind, the sold-out reset). `helpers.ts`, `updates.test.ts` and `order-safety.test.ts` were adjusted.

**Customer side**
- **`src/lib/kitchen.ts`:** the one wording helper (notice, button, strip, sold-out line, checkout error, pickup slots). `kitchen-server.ts` reads the status once per request. `KitchenProvider` re-reads it on focus, on visibility change and at the next clock change, using the server's clock offset, so a wrong phone clock never matters.
- **Menu:** shows the closed notice, and the quiet "Last orders at …" line in the last 30 minutes before last orders. Sold-out regulars say "Back tomorrow" or "Back Monday".
- **Main buttons:** while closed, the cart, checkout and order panel show the disabled "Order from 11:00 tomorrow · KSh …" button.
- **Checkout:** pickup chips come from today's real hours (the first 3 plus Later). The kitchen closing mid-checkout gives "We've just closed. Opens … Your order is saved."
- **Order page:** till line "M-Pesa till: 123456 · Copy" on unpaid orders.

**Dashboard**
- **Shell** (`nav.tsx`, `layout.tsx`): rail from 1024 px, tab bar below it, More page; Settings is owner only. The new-orders badge shows 99+ and "!" when the count fails. Skip link, `themeColor #fbf7f1` (checked in the rendered HTML), print hides the shell.
- **Closed-early strip:** shown on every dashboard screen except Settings (see Decisions).
- **Settings** (`src/app/dashboard/settings/`):
  - status strip;
  - Close early card with a confirm step and the pickups-due line;
  - weekly hours with switches, the copy-hours link, last orders and a sticky save bar;
  - closed dates with Undo and the planned-dishes note;
  - till number with a confirm step;
  - a read-only view for staff.
- **Dishes:** "Sold out" uses `set_regular_on_sale` and shows "Back on sale tomorrow."

    - the till confirm's "Save till number" no longer wraps at 375 (Go back sized to content);
    - the strip and closed-early banner repetition was already fixed: the shared strip is hidden on Settings.
  - **Not changed (spec decisions, for dev.designer or Tevin):**
    - (P1) seven always-editable day rows make hours about 1,100 px tall on a phone; the reviewer suggests a "Mon–Sun 11:00–20:00 · Edit" summary;
    - the save bar covers the next row while editing (the spec places it sticky above the tab bar);
    - staff don't see Settings in the nav (as specified);
    - midnight is entered as 00:00 (spec 2A), with the hint under the hours.
- **`impeccable` audit:** about **16/20, Good**.
  - **A11y 3:** labelled fields, `role=switch` and `aria-checked`, focus moves on every confirm step, polite announcements, errors tied by `aria-describedby`, skip link. Contrast was not machine-measured.
  - **Performance 4:** no layout animation.
  - **Responsive 3:** no sideways scroll at 1280, 768 or 375; 44–48 px targets; 16 px inputs.
  - **Theming 3.**
  - **Implementation integrity 3:** the detector found only 7 font-size advisories. I fixed 22→24 (tile headings), 17→16 and 12→13. Kept deliberately: the 34 px h1, which matches the other dashboard pages (existing drift, not new), the 11.5 px badge, and the 12 px tab labels from the shell spec.
- **`review-animations`:** checked against the spec's Motion table. Only the listed motion exists:
  - press `scale(.97)`;
  - the switch knob `translateX(18px)`, with the track colour instant;
  - `note-in` (6 px, `--dur-small`, `--ease-out`) on the confirm steps, results, added or undone rows and errors;
  - the strip enters from 8 px above and exits the same way, not on page load.
  - All use tokens. `prefers-reduced-motion` keeps opacity only and moves the knob instantly. Nothing exceeds 300 ms, and there is no `transition: all`.
- **`mobile-native`:**
  - tab and rail items have `touch-action: manipulation`, no tap highlight, no callout and no text selection; hover only on fine pointers;
  - the tab bar hides while a field is focused on touch screens;
  - safe areas are handled; inputs are 16 px; native time and date pickers are full width with `min-width: 0`.

**What changed**
1. **Phone hours list** (`settings/hours-form.tsx`): below 600 px each day is one 56 px row (name, Today pill, the hours as text, switch). Rows read "11:00 – midnight". In `kitchen.ts` every last-orders time now goes through `closingTime()` (quiet line, "Last orders were at", open-now line, closed strip), as do pickup times on the order page and the order board.
