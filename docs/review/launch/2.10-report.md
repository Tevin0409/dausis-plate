## Design calls made in the build

These are for QA, and for Tevin where noted.
- **Star outline:** the empty star's outline uses `--field` (#8c8478), not sand. Sand failed 3:1 against paper.
- **Anon select on `order_ratings`:** it is refused with 42501 rather than returning an empty list. Neither leaks anything.
- **Totals trigger:** `guard_dish_rating_totals` stops anyone from writing a dish's rating totals directly. Only the rating functions change them.
- **Paging** uses `sent_at` (keyset), not offsets, so a rating arriving mid-scroll doesn't shift the list.
- **Undo after "Remove reply"** sends the reply again. `replied_at` becomes now, and History shows "replied" after "removed". This is honest, but the reply's time changes.
- **History wording:** the Google link line is one sentence, "changed the Google review link.", without the old and new link.
- **Owner actions (from the critique):** "Reply" / "Edit reply" is the same quiet pill as the others, but in teal, so it reads first. "Hide from stars" sits at the end of the row; at 375 it wraps to its own line. Both stay quiet, as the spec asks.
- **Summary line:** "30 days · 3 hidden" uses no-break spaces, so a wrap never starts a line with "·".

## Motion (`review-animations`, against spec Motion)

Every row in the Motion table is built as specified:
- The star press is `scale(.94)` at `--dur-press`.
- Form to thanks is a crossfade: out on `--dur-exit`, in on opacity plus `translateY(6px)` on `--dur-small`, via `@starting-style`. It runs only after a send in this tab.
- Hide and Show change opacity from 1 to .6 on `--dur-small` with `--ease-in-out`.
- A reply just sent, and the "Show 30 more" cards, fade in (opacity only, no stagger).
- The filter switch and the reply box are instant.

General rules held throughout:
- Only `transform` and `opacity` animate, never `transition: all` and never ease-in.
- Under reduced motion, there is no scale or translate, only opacity.
- Hover is gated by `(hover: hover) and (pointer: fine)`.

**One finding, fixed:** the swap after the fade-out used a hard-coded 240 ms. It now reads `--dur-exit` from the theme, plus 20 ms. Verdict: approve.
